# ☕ Starbucks Sales Analysis Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive **Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze Starbucks sales and transaction data, uncover sales patterns, and provide actionable insights into **revenue performance, customer activity, order trends, and operational performance**.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw Starbucks transaction data into a business-focused analytical solution that helps stakeholders understand:

* How sales are performing over time
* When sales activity is highest
* How customer transactions contribute to overall performance
* Which products and categories drive sales
* Where opportunities exist to improve sales and operational efficiency

## 📊 Key Business Metrics

The dashboard provides an overview of important sales KPIs, including:

* **Total Sales / Revenue**
* **Total Quantity Sold**
* **Total Transactions / Orders**
* **Average Sales Performance**
* **Sales Trends Over Time**
* **Hourly Sales Performance**

These metrics provide stakeholders with a consolidated view of overall business performance.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 📈 Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzed sales patterns across different time periods to identify changes in revenue and transaction activity.

### ⏰ Hourly Sales Analysis

Analyzed sales performance by hour to identify **peak and low-demand periods**, helping businesses better understand customer purchasing patterns and potential opportunities for operational planning.

### 🧾 Transaction Analysis

Examined transaction-level data to understand order volumes, purchasing activity, and sales contribution.

### ☕ Product & Category Analysis

Analyzed product and category performance to identify products contributing significantly to overall sales and understand demand patterns.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps stakeholders:

* Identify **peak sales periods** and customer demand patterns
* Understand transaction and revenue trends
* Evaluate product and category performance
* Identify potential opportunities to improve sales
* Support operational and resource planning using historical sales patterns
* Make faster, **data-driven business decisions**
